Our Mission

Make restaurant communication faster, calmer, and more profitable

Restaurants lose valuable revenue when incoming calls are dropped, guests are put on long hold queues, or waitstaff must constantly run away from tables to answer repetitive phone questions. Xorvian was founded by operations experts specifically to solve this bottleneck.
